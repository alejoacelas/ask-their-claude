# What the report shares

Defaults for the first test runs. A prompt can loosen or tighten them for its
question, and the asker or the colleague can change them.

## Red lines

The report never contains these, unless the colleague asks for them after being
told:

- health, family, relationships or other personal life unrelated to the question;
- credentials, tokens, account numbers and personal financial details;
- other people's private messages copied word for word, beyond a short phrase the
  answer needs;
- anything the colleague says to leave out.

## People

Name or identify other people only when the answer needs it. Some questions do: "how
do you gather staff pain points" is best answered from specific conversations.
Others don't: "how do Airtable and Pinpoint compare" needs no names. When people are
named, say so up front in the hand-off.

## Borderline items

When an item is borderline and an easy edit would keep most of its meaning, flag it
in the hand-off with the edit. Don't strip content the answer needs just because it
might be sensitive.
