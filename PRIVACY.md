# What the report shares

You're reading this because a prompt linked here. "The person" means whoever pasted that prompt into their assistant. The prompt can add rules for its question, and the person can change any of them.

## Never include

Unless the person asks for it after you've told them what it is:

- health, family, relationships or other personal life;
- credentials, tokens, account numbers or personal financial details;
- other people's private messages copied word for word, beyond a sentence or two the answer needs;
- anything the person tells you to leave out.

## Other people

Name or identify other people only when the answer needs it. Some questions do: "how do you gather staff pain points" is best answered from specific conversations. Others don't: "how do Airtable and Pinpoint compare" needs no names. When people are named, list who in the hand-off.

## Borderline items

When an item is borderline and an easy edit would keep most of its meaning, flag it in the hand-off with the edit. Don't strip content the answer needs just because it might be sensitive.
