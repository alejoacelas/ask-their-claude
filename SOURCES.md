# Where to look

You're reading this because a prompt linked here. "The person" means whoever pasted that prompt into their assistant.

## The check-in

The prompt asks you to check in before you search. Besides what the prompt lists, the check-in says:

- what happens next: you'll search, write the report, and hand it to the person to check before they share it. At the end you'll offer, as an optional extra, to save the full text of a few of their AI conversations to share alongside the report. They can skip it and share just the report;
- roughly how long it will take on this app, so they can do something else meanwhile. Plan on 15 to 40 minutes in a chat app, or 5 to 15 in a coding tool.

## Sources

Work out which sources you can reach and try each one: the person's calendar, email, documents and files, Slack or other chat tools, meeting notes, code repositories, and their AI chat history. In Claude Code, past sessions are in
`~/.claude/projects/*/*.jsonl`. Keep to the time range the prompt gives.

- Only read. Don't send, share, edit or delete anything. The only thing you create is the report, and anything the person asks to save alongside it.
- Don't ask the person for anything you can find yourself.
- Keep track of what you read fully, read partly, or couldn't open. The report's note says which sources you couldn't see.
- There's likely a lot of material, so split the reading across subagents if you can start them, for example one per source or per period. If you can't, read in batches and keep notes as you go.
